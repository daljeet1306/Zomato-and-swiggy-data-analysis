# Research Methodology

## Research Approach

This study uses a comparative and descriptive approach to examine the financial and food-delivery operational performance of Eternal Limited and Swiggy Limited.

## Data Sources

Secondary data was collected from the companies’ official annual reports, financial statements, and investor-relations documents. Official company data was used as the primary source.

Artificial intelligence was used to assist in locating, extracting, organizing, and explaining information from the reports. The extracted figures were checked against the original reports for accuracy.

## Period of Study

The study covers financial years ending March 31, based on the years for which relevant official data was available. Missing information is marked as N/A and was not estimated.

## Indicators Used

Financial indicators include:

- Revenue
- Total income
- Total expenses
- Profit or loss
- EBITDA
- Adjusted EBITDA

Operational indicators include:

- Food-delivery GOV
- Number of orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Monthly transacting users or customers
- Contribution margin
- Adjusted EBITDA margin

## Data Analysis

The financial and operational data is organized into separate datasets. The companies are compared using revenue growth, expenses, profitability, GOV, order volume, AOV, customer activity, and operating margins.

The findings are used to develop practical recommendations for improving sales, customer engagement, operational efficiency, and profitability.

## Limitations

The comparison may be affected by missing data, differences in reporting terminology, changes in metric definitions, and the use of adjusted financial measures.
