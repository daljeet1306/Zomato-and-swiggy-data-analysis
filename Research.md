Research questions

	1. What factors influence the revenue and sales growth of Eternal Limited and Swiggy Limited, and what strategies could help them increase food-delivery sales?
	2. How can Eternal and Swiggy improve customer retention and increase the frequency of orders among existing users?
	3. How do GOV, order volume, and average order value differ between the two companies, and what actions could help them increase order value and total transaction volume?
	4. How can the companies improve their food-delivery contribution margins by reducing costs and increasing operational efficiency?
	5. What do the companies’ expenses, losses, EBITDA, and adjusted EBITDA margins indicate about their profitability challenges, and how can they move toward sustainable profitability?
	6. Based on the financial and operational comparison, which business strategies should Eternal and Swiggy prioritize to achieve stronger sales growth, customer engagement, and long-term profitability?
