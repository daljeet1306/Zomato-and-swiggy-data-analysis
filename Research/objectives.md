# Research Objectives

1. To compare the financial performance of Eternal Limited and Swiggy Limited using official annual-report data.

2. To examine and compare the food-delivery operational performance of both companies.

3. To identify factors affecting revenue, order volume, customer activity, and profitability.

4. To compare the companies’ expenses, operating margins, and profitability challenges.

5. To develop practical, data-supported recommendations for improving sales, customer engagement, and operational efficiency.

6. To identify the limitations caused by missing data and differences in reporting methods.
