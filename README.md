# Comparative Financial and Operational Analysis of Eternal Limited and Swiggy Limited

## Project Overview

This project compares the financial and food-delivery operational performance of Eternal Limited and Swiggy Limited using official company data.

The study examines revenue, expenses, profitability, food-delivery GOV, order volume, average order value, customer activity, contribution margin, and adjusted EBITDA margin.

The purpose is to identify important business trends and develop practical recommendations for improving sales, customer engagement, operational efficiency, and long-term profitability.

## Research Questions

1. What factors influence the revenue and sales growth of Eternal Limited and Swiggy Limited, and what strategies could help them increase food-delivery sales?

2. How can Eternal and Swiggy improve customer retention and increase the frequency of orders among existing users?

3. How do GOV, order volume, and average order value differ between the two companies, and what actions could help them increase order value and total transaction volume?

4. How can the companies improve their food-delivery contribution margins by reducing costs and increasing operational efficiency?

5. What do the companies’ expenses, losses, EBITDA, and adjusted EBITDA margins indicate about their profitability challenges, and how can they move toward sustainable profitability?

6. Based on the financial and operational comparison, which business strategies should Eternal and Swiggy prioritize to achieve stronger sales growth, customer engagement, and long-term profitability?

## Objectives

- To compare the financial performance of Eternal and Swiggy.
- To compare their food-delivery operational performance.
- To identify factors affecting sales, customer activity, and profitability.
- To develop practical, data-supported recommendations.
- To identify the limitations of the available data.

## Data Sources

The study uses official annual reports, financial statements, and investor-relations documents published by Eternal Limited and Swiggy Limited.

Artificial intelligence was used to assist in extracting and organizing information from the reports. The extracted data was checked against the original documents.

## Project Structure

- `Research/` — Research questions, objectives, methodology, and key terms.
- `data/` — Financial and operational datasets.
- `reports/` — Official report links and source information.
- `analysis/` — Financial analysis, operational analysis, and recommendations.
- `final-report/` — Complete final report.

## Scope and Limitations

The analysis covers the financial years for which relevant official data was available. Missing values are marked as N/A.

The comparison may be affected by differences in reporting terminology, changes in metric definitions, missing years, and the use of adjusted financial measures.
